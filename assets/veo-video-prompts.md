# VEO 3.1 Video Prompts for Klarpakke Kurs

## ⚠️ VIKTIG: IKKE INKLUDER TEKST
VEO klarer IKKE å generere lesbar tekst. Bruk kun visuelle symboler, ikoner og grafikk.

---

## 🎯 Alternativ A: AI Professor Hologram (ANBEFALT)
**Varighet:** 8-10 sekunder  
**Stil:** Futuristisk, glassmorphism, cyan/blå/lilla  
**Format:** 16:9

### Prompt:
```
A friendly minimalist robot avatar with large expressive cyan-blue eyes 
and a warm smile materializes from swirling holographic particles. 
The robot has a translucent glass-like body with soft internal glow. 
Cyan and purple light particles dance around it. Background is deep 
navy blue with subtle floating geometric shapes. Soft volumetric 
lighting, cinematic 8k quality. The robot waves gently at camera with 
one hand. Glassmorphism aesthetic with frosted glass effects. No text, 
no letters, no numbers, no words anywhere in the scene.
```

### Motion beskrivelse:
- 0-2s: Particles swirling, forming the avatar
- 2-6s: Avatar fully formed, gentle floating/breathing motion
- 6-8s: Avatar waves, warm smile
- 8-10s: Gentle fade or loop back

## 🎓 Alternativ B: "Fra Forvirring til Klarhet" Trailer
**Varighet:** 20-25 sekunder  
**Stil:** Motion graphics, glassmorphism

### Scene 1: Kaos (0-5s)
```
Abstract visualization of information overload: Chaotic floating red 
charts, confusing jagged lines, overwhelming data streams. A small 
silhouette figure stands overwhelmed. Dark moody blue background. 
Glass panels shatter or glitch. No text or words, only abstract 
shapes and symbols. Cinematic lighting.
```

### Scene 2: Guiding Light (5-12s)
```
A warm golden-blue light beam cuts through the chaos. The silhouette 
starts walking toward the light. Floating glass cards with simple 
white icons appear along the path: a coin symbol, a shield symbol, 
a graduation cap symbol. Clean geometric shapes only, no text. 
Purple to blue gradient background. Smooth camera movement.
```

### Scene 3: Mestring (12-20s)
```
The figure now stands confident on a glowing platform of glass 
hexagons. A clean, floating holographic dashboard appears with 
green upward arrows and golden sparkles. The friendly robot avatar 
from Scene 1 appears beside them, giving a thumbs up. Warm, inviting 
lighting. No text or labels, only glowing UI elements and symbols.
```

### Scene 4: Logo Reveal (20-25s)
```
Clean glassmorphism background. A stylized letter K logo made of 
light particles materializes and glows cyan. Abstract geometric 
shapes float elegantly. No words, just the glowing K symbol and 
soft particle effects. Premium Apple-style aesthetic.
```

## 🛠️ Tekniske Tips for VEO 3.1

### Beste innstillinger:
- **Aspect ratio:** 16:9 (1920x1080)
- **Duration:** 8-10 sekunder = best kvalitet
- **Seed:** Bruk samme seed for konsistent stil
- **Negative prompt:** ALLTID inkluder "no text, no words, no letters, no numbers"

### Fargepalett:
```
Background: #0f172a (mørk blå)
Primary:    #3b82f6 (cyan blå)
Secondary:  #a855f7 (lilla)
Accent:     #06b6d4 (turkis)
Gold:       #f59e0b (gul/oransje)
Green:      #10b981 (suksess)
```

### Keywords som fungerer bra:
- "glassmorphism"
- "frosted glass material"
- "holographic particles"
- "volumetric lighting"
- "8k cinematic render"
- "UI/UX motion design"
- "Apple product style"
- "seamless loop"
- "abstract geometric"
- "glowing edges"
- "translucent surfaces"

## 📋 Step-by-Step VEO Guide

1. **Gå til:** https://labs.google/fx/tools/video-fx

2. **Velg:** VEO 3.1 (høyeste kvalitet)

3. **Innstillinger:**
   - Aspect ratio: 16:9
   - Duration: 8-10s
   - Quality: High

4. **Lim inn prompt** (husk: INGEN tekst!)

5. **Negative prompt:**
   ```
   text, words, letters, numbers, typography, font, written language, 
   blurry, low quality, distorted faces
   ```

6. **Generer 4 varianter**, velg beste

7. **Eksporter** som MP4

8. **Plassér** i `/public/videos/`
